Initial release (0.1.0):
- Race your routes against a ghost of your personal best with a live AHEAD/BEHIND gap.
- Record runs with live distance, pace and time; see splits, results and run history.
- Route library with stats, route/run replay, and GPX import/export.
- Map view with per-route offline maps on supported device builds.
- Bundled example route to explore, dark/light themes, and on-device storage (no account, no cloud).
