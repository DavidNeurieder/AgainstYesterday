Against Yesterday is a GPS run/cycling app built around one idea: every route
is a race with your own personal best. You run against a "ghost" of your PB,
and the live gap — AHEAD or BEHIND — is the hero metric.

Features:
- Record runs with live distance, pace, elapsed time and PB gap.
- Ghost racing on the map with YOU and PB-ghost markers.
- Route library with PB/average/last stats and performance charts.
- Offline maps (per-route downloads, OpenStreetMap).
- GPX export to Downloads.
- Background recording with persisted snapshots.

Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Map style by OpenFreeMap.
