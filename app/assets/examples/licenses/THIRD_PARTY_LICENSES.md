## Cycling Helsinki

- Source: GeoPrivacy Open Data (2023)
- Citation: Mäkinen, V., Brauer, A. and Oksanen, J. 2023. GeoPrivacy platform,
  available at: https://geoprivacy.fi
- Acknowledgement: "We made use of geospatial data provided by the Open
  Geospatial Information Infrastructure for Research (Geoportti,
  urn:nbn:fi:research-infras-2016072513) funded by the Academy of Finland,
  CSC – IT Center for Science, and other Geoportti consortium members."
- License: CC BY 4.0
- Attribution string shown in-app: "GPS data: GeoPrivacy (2023) | CC BY 4.0"

The GeoPrivacy platform is a service where cyclists, joggers and pedestrians
donate GPS/GNSS tracking data for science; the open data repository provides
privacy-protected versions of those tracks. Tracks are truncated/shifted for
privacy and only cover Finland in densely populated areas.
